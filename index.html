Research and education on how voters engage with the democratic process.

About Us

A 501(c)(3) research and education nonprofit.

The Foundation for Campaign Innovation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We research how voters are contacted, informed, and turned out, and we share what we learn through training and educational resources.

Our Work

Three lines of research.

01

Polling & Survey Research

We conduct and sponsor surveys, focus groups, and in-depth interviews. They look at how voters interact with elected officials, how they use technology to follow the news and make decisions, and what they think about key policy issues. We make these findings public, especially for elected officials, nonprofit leaders, and the media.

02

Digital Readiness

We track how campaigns adopt digital communication and new technologies. Our approach is nonpartisan and draws on our survey research.

03

Original Field Research

We run and sponsor field studies on topics such as website usability, public policy messaging, reproducing academic research, and the effects of AI regulation.

Education & Training

We turn research into resources people can use.

We turn our research into newsletters, blog posts, podcasts, and webinars for elected officials, policy professionals, and engaged citizens. We also work with partners on in-person and online training.
